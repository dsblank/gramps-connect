Gramps Connect

A fast, collaborative way to work on a Gramps family tree in your browser, even a very large one, alone or with family.

Download for your computer Host it for your family Read the documentation

What it is

Gramps Connect works on a tree served by Gramps Web API, the Gramps Project's own server. It follows the layout and vocabulary of Gramps itself, so if you've used Gramps before it should feel familiar.

Fast on small and huge trees

Browsing, sorting and filtering stay quick on trees with tens of thousands of people, because the parts of the tree you're looking at are kept right in your browser.

Work together, live

Several people can edit one tree at once and see each other's changes as they happen. Every record keeps a history of who changed what.

Charts, maps and stories

Ancestor, descendant and fan charts; maps with historical basemaps and old-map overlays; generated life stories, a family blog and photo tagging.

Search your way

Search the whole tree at once, build filters by picking rules from a list, or write queries directly, and save the filters you use often.

Edit everything

Full editing forms for people, families, events, places and more, stacked so you can create a missing record without leaving the one you're on. Drop photos anywhere to attach them.

Familiar to Gramps users

The same views, record types and vocabulary as Gramps, with names, dates and places written in your tree's chosen formats. Settings can be imported straight from Gramps Desktop.

Scripting with Gramplets

Write small Python Gramplets that run right in your browser to add your own views, charts and reports, or install ones others have shared from the Gramplet Store.

Many languages

The interface comes in nearly fifty languages, dates are written the way each language writes them, and the documentation is being translated too.

Import and export

Bring a tree in from a Gramps XML or GEDCOM file and take it back out again whenever you like, so your data is never locked in.

Two ways to use it

On your own computer

The desktop build for Windows, macOS and Linux runs entirely on your machine, and nothing you do in it is sent anywhere. Installing

Shared on a server

A Docker deployment lets a whole family sign in and work on one tree together, which is where live collaboration comes in. Deploying

Gramps Connect is under active development and is not an official release of the Gramps Project. Keep your tree backed up. For a mature, feature-complete option today, see Gramps Desktop.
