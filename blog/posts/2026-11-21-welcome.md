Gramps Connect now has a blog. This is where new releases, new features,
and notes on how things work behind the scenes will be posted. You can
follow along here, or subscribe to the [feed](feed.xml) in any feed reader.

## Take the tour

If you haven't tried Gramps Connect yet, the quickest way to see what it
does is the new [narrated tour](../tour/). It walks through a real family
tree (Abraham Lincoln's) and shows the main views: lists and columns,
filters, charts, maps, stories, working together with others, and
Gramplets.

![The People list searched for "lincoln", with Abraham Lincoln's details on the right](img/tour-lincoln.jpg)
*The tour starts at Abraham Lincoln, in the People list.*
